Our Journey

Five years of lighting young minds.

What began on 11 June 2022 as a tribute to Uma Lakshmi has become an annual promise kept to a growing family of students. From thirty children in one Chennai school to seventy-five across ten districts, every year has brought more students, more schools, and more families who no longer have to choose between fees and food.

265 students sponsored · 21 schools and 9 colleges · 10 districts of Tamil Nadu · five annual sponsorship functions.

Year by year

The milestones so far.

Founded 11 June 2022

The first thirty students

Within a month of founding, notebooks and stationery reached 80 children at CSI School, Mandaveli. On 10 September the first sponsorship function at Adyar KB Nagar RA Club awarded cheques to 30 students from government-aided schools, in the presence of headmistress Mrs. Amutha, Dr. V. Bhaskar and Dr. N. Veerappan.

30 April 2023 · RA Puram

Reaching beyond Chennai

The second function at Rani Meyyammai Girls Higher Secondary School welcomed Dr. Anantha Valli of CSIR as chief guest. Forty-six students were sponsored, and for the first time cheques were posted to outstation students in other districts.

22 June 2024 · RA Puram

Growing for the third year

Fifty-one students received sponsorship at the third annual function. The founder spoke of continuous growth in student strength and thanked Mrs. Parvathi Murugappan for her dedicated support to the trust's work.

June 2025 · 12A & 80G received

Recognised and registered

Sixty-three students from schools and colleges in ten districts were sponsored at the fourth function. The same year the trust received its permanent registration under Section 12A and approval under Section 80G, so that every donor's gift is now tax-deductible.

2026 · Fifth anniversary

Seventy-five students, and counting

The fifth annual sponsorship programme marks five years of the trust and its largest cohort yet — 75 students — bringing the total to 265 students supported since 2022. [Date, venue and highlights to be added after the function.]

Our activities

More than fees.

Fee sponsorship is the heart of the trust's work, but it is not all of it. Alongside the annual programme, the trust responds to needs as they arise.

01

Fee sponsorship

Annual school and college fees for 265 students over five years, paid directly to 30 institutions.

02

Study materials

Notebooks and stationery for around 100 children at CSI Elementary School, Mylapore, beginning in the trust's first month.

03

Children's home support

Living and study materials supplied to a school for orphaned and abandoned children.

04

Full support for girls

Three girl children fully sponsored for food, clothing and education together.
